About

Hi, I'm Chandra — a UI/UX designer and creative strategist with 3.5+ years of experience across e-commerce, healthcare, B2B and AI-driven products. I started at Digital Pitaa, designing e-commerce platforms, a healthcare booking experience and a B2B marketplace — work that taught me how differently “simple” looks depending on who's using it.

From there I moved to Vedak, redesigning a client-expert matching platform and an enterprise CRM suite, building a 150+ component design system along the way and baking accessibility (WCAG 2.1) into the process rather than bolting it on after.

These days I'm at Graphene, designing AI agent interfaces for Fortune 500 FMCG brands — turning complex automation workflows into dashboards non-technical teams can actually use, alongside the visual branding and campaign work that goes with it.

If you're building something that needs to make a complicated system feel obvious, I'd like to hear about it.
