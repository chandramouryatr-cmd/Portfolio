About

Hi, I'm Ava — a product designer based in Lisbon. It started with a broken parking-app flow I redesigned for fun during a bootcamp and couldn't stop tinkering with. That itch turned into a job at a twelve-person fintech startup, where I learned that good design is mostly about saying no.

Since then I've worked across fintech, health and civic tech — always chasing the same question: what's the smallest number of decisions someone has to make to get where they're going?

These days I split my time between client work and a slow-burn design system side project called Keystone, which mostly exists to give me an excuse to obsess over spacing tokens.

If you're building something that needs to feel simple and doesn't yet, I'd like to hear about it.
